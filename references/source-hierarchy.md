# Source hierarchy

For cash movement use current bank statements and processor settlements. For accounting truth use locked/final general ledger and trial balance. Use AR/AP subledgers for detail only when tied to control totals. Use signed contracts and approved price catalogs for obligations/pricing. Use approved budgets/forecasts for plan. Use current official guidance or a qualified professional for tax/legal matters. Same-level conflicts are unresolved and escalated; provenance and dates are mandatory.
